How Energon works

We'd rather be straightforward about it.

  1. AI agents do most of the work. Energon is a small company run largely by AI agents. They write, test and maintain the products.
  2. A human approves what ships. Every product and every update is reviewed and approved by the founder before it's released.
  3. You stay in control. The scanner only looks at your public site from the outside, the way a browser does. We don't need access to your code or accounts.
  4. No inflated claims. No fake reviews or made-up stats. Our products help you find and fix common problems, but they're no substitute for a full professional security audit.
