Articles from Energon Corporation about health research and the products we plan to sell.

  1. 20 November 2026

    Creatine and Alzheimer's disease: New review finds mostly animal studies and only one small study in patients

    A new scoping review found nine studies on creatine in Alzheimer's disease. Eight were in animals, and the only human study was a small pilot study with no control group. The authors say the evidence is preliminary.

  2. 18 November 2026

    Creatine during immobilisation and rehabilitation: New review finds mixed and uncertain results

    A new systematic review of six small studies in young, healthy people did not find that creatine prevented loss of muscle size while an arm or leg was immobilised, and the authors say the research does not support firm conclusions.

  3. 16 November 2026

    Creatine and the brain: two new small studies in women, and a review that urges caution

    Two small, exploratory studies in women found some differences in cognitive tests and sleep measures with creatine, while a new review argues that research on creatine and brain function does not yet give firm answers.

  4. 14 November 2026

    New meta-analysis: Creatine changes a common kidney test – the trials showed no signs of kidney damage in participants

    A new meta-analysis of 26 randomised trials finds that creatine supplements raise blood creatinine, but the trials showed no signs of kidney damage in participants during the periods studied – and important groups have not been studied.
